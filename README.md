# METUBite
A campus dining and price-performance rating guide concept for METU students.

**METUBite** is a student-centered campus dining guide and rating platform concept designed for **Middle East Technical University (METU / ODTÜ)**. It helps students discover campus canteens and cafés, highlight must-try dishes, and compare real price-performance ratios across campus.

---

## Problem

On a large campus with dozens of canteens, cafés, and dining halls, students often struggle to answer three simple questions between classes:
- *Where can I get the best meal for my budget right now?*
- *Which specific dishes are actually worth ordering at each canteen?*
- *How do portion sizes and prices compare across different departments?*

---

## Proposed Solution

**METUBite** brings transparency and peer recommendations to campus dining through three core features:

1. **Interactive Campus Dining Map:** Visual overview of canteens and cafés across METU departments, showing walking distance, hours, and crowd levels.
2. **Price-Performance (P/P) Scoring:** Dedicated student ratings evaluating portion size, taste, and affordability rather than generic star ratings.
3. **Favorite Dish Highlights:** Community-voted top dishes for every venue so students know exactly what to order before waiting in line.

---

## Target Audience & Value

- **METU Students:** Save money, avoid disappointing meals, and discover hidden-gem spots across different faculties.
- **Campus Venues:** Gain clear feedback on popular menu items and student expectations.

---

## Future Roadmap (If Developed)

- **Phase 1:** Crowdsourced campus venue database and price-performance rating system.
- **Phase 2:** Daily cafeteria menu integration and real-time queue/crowd indicators.
- **Phase 3:** Personalized recommendations based on department location and daily budget.
